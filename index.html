◈ VISION & GESTURE CONTROL
LIVE VISION IN CONVO MODE
Merges the camera into hands-free CONVO — front camera stays on so you can just say "analyze" (e.g. "analyze what I'm wearing", "analyze this gesture", "analyze what I'm holding") and it'll look and answer out loud.
GESTURE MOUSE (RIGHT HAND)
Tracks your right hand as a pointer: pinch (thumb+index) to click/select, make a fist and move to drag panels around, hold fingers extended together, pointing up to scroll up, or a loose curled hand pointing down to scroll down. Bring both hands together to zoom in on whatever's under them (a panel, or the hologram if AIRGLASS is open), or pull them apart to zoom out — say "Ultra, stop zoom" any time to lock zoom off, and "Ultra, start zoom" to turn it back on. Needs camera + mic access and a modern browser. While active, a single finger snap slows the cursor (and the hologram's rotation) down for fine control (snap again to return to normal speed), and a double snap jumps straight into conversation mode — double snap again while already in conversation mode to exit it. While AIRGLASS is open, that same hand also drives any loaded hologram: point your hand up/down/left/right and it spins that direction endlessly, make a fist to grab it and stop the spin (then move your fisted hand to rotate it slowly), and bring your thumb and index finger together to grow it, or apart to shrink it when only one hand is in frame.

◈ WHAT THIS APP CANNOT DO
This is a chat interface — even in ONLINE mode. It cannot log into your email or other accounts, take actions on other websites, build and deploy an app end-to-end without you running commands, spend your money, or run a business autonomously. Any of that requires you to connect specific services (with your permission, per service) and, for real software/business projects, actual step-by-step work — which I'm glad to help you plan and execute, just not something a webpage can silently do in the background.
